# Palette - UX/Visual Learnings

## 2025-02-22 - Premium Industrial Logistics Aesthetic
**Learning:** For B2B industrial logistics (cold chain), a "Premium Industrial" look is achieved by combining a deep corporate blue (#102a51) with vibrant tech-accents (Azul Eficaz #266bd9 and Celeste Frío #63c0da).
**Action:** Use glassmorphism (backdrop-blur) on fixed headers and floating sidebars to maintain a modern, transparent feel without losing legibility.
**Action:** Implement grayscale-to-color hover effects on fleet galleries to emphasize "bringing life/freshness" to the logistics process.
**Action:** Use high-contrast badges (Checkmarks) for immediate trust verification in the hero section.
